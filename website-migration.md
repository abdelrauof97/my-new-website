<section class="page-section">
  <div class="container narrow stack">
    <p>Most of the people who call about a new website already have one. It may be on Real Geeks, BoomTown, Sierra Interactive, Luxury Presence, WordPress, or something assembled years ago. The audience, the content, and the domain are the business. The platform is not.</p>
    <p>RealtorToThrive migrates established real estate websites. Real Geeks migrations are a particular focus: the design language, the pages, and the lead paths agents have trained their marketing to use. Other real estate platforms are in scope when the goal is the same. Leave with a faster, more ownable site, and keep the identity that already converts.</p>
    <p>A move can still affect search visibility. We do not promise that it will not. We plan the change so the effect is measured and intentional.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="page-section section--mist">
  <div class="container">
    <h2>What the migration covers</h2>
    <div class="pillar-grid">
      <article>
        <h3>Assessment</h3>
        <p>We review the current site: templates, content worth keeping, listing setup, forms, and the URLs your marketing already points at.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>Design choice</h3>
        <p>Preserve the design your clients recognize, or replace it. Both are valid. The decision is made before pages are rebuilt.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>Content and listings</h3>
        <p>Pages and written content move with the site. Property listings move where the source and the destination support it.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>Search continuity</h3>
        <p>Redirect maps, metadata kept where it still describes the page, and a clean URL structure. Validation before the domain cuts over.</p>
      </article>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
