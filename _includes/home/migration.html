Migration

Ready for a better platform? Bring your website's identity with you.

Switching platforms shouldn't mean starting over. We help real estate professionals transition their websites, content, and digital presence while preserving the identity they've worked hard to build.

Real Geeks migrations are a specialty. The same care applies when the current site runs on BoomTown, Sierra Interactive, Luxury Presence, WordPress, or a similar platform. Some practices want the existing design carried forward. Others use the move as the moment for a complete redesign. Both are planned, not improvised.

What a careful move includes

  • An assessment of the current website
  • Design preservation or a complete redesign
  • Page and content migration
  • Property listing migration where the platform supports it
  • URL and redirect planning
  • SEO metadata preserved where it still belongs
  • Domain transition assistance
  • CRM and lead-capture integrations
  • Testing and launch support

We do not promise zero impact on search. A platform change can move rankings. The job is to plan the redirects, check the templates, and keep the disruption as small as the project allows.
