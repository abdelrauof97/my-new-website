<section class="page-section" id="performance">
  <div class="container narrow stack">
    <h2>Performance-first development</h2>
    <p>Speed is a build constraint. Templates are designed mobile-first, images are cropped to the space they fill, and modern formats are used so a phone is not downloading a desktop photograph. JavaScript is added when a page needs it.</p>
    <p>Before launch we review Core Web Vitals, image weight, and the cost of any third-party script. Those reviews are engineering checks. They are not a promise that a public test will show a particular score after your pixels, chat widget, and tag manager are in place.</p>
    <ul class="check-list">
      <li>Fast-loading templates and restrained page weight</li>
      <li>Responsive images in WebP or AVIF, with dimensions set</li>
      <li>Mobile-first layout and touch-friendly actions</li>
      <li>Lightweight markup instead of a stacked theme</li>
      <li>Performance tested against the real templates, not a blank demo</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</section>
